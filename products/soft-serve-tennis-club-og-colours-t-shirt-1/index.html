Soft Serve Tennis Club was the design that started this venture.
Originally created as a Christmas present for a group of friends that played tennis at the Wick Park tennis courts weekly, I wanted a way to be able to print these off whenever I required one.
The design adapts the logo of an Australian soft serve company to humourous effect.
This is the spooky colour variation of the original. We all love a little bit of spook.
